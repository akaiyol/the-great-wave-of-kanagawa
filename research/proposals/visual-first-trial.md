Status: proposed process trial, not a proven efficiency improvement. Owner approved trial and requires a worth-it decision immediately after the next art iteration. This does not reopen the completed until-90%-usage run.

## Scope and sequence

Hold the P084 composition fixed. The next art experiment targets the shared crest boundary: replace smooth foam cap and sparse hooks together with connected unequal foam groups, water coves and open sky. Lead builds uncertain geometry; an optional worker receives only a defined patch. Same evaluator role challenges the premise before work and judges the rendered outcome; researcher only for an exact blocker. One child active and one writer. Preserve existing models and strategy/phase gates.

Declare one predicted visible change and its falsifier before implementation. Make a disposable code-generated crest study; use only loading/finite-output checks needed to inspect it safely. A promising result earns affected integrity tests and integration review; no unchecked prototype is promoted. The final 5% process review includes the required trial verdict.

## Mandatory worth-it decision after this one art iteration

Use retain / modify / reject / inconclusive, with evidence and next action. Do not advance to another art phase until the verdict is recorded in that phase's review.

Retain provisionally only if the predicted visible gain is independently supported, no material regression survives required checks, and measured effort or specific avoided work supports keeping the sequence. A fast failed experiment can support the early-rejection mechanism, but does not establish faster progress toward accepted artwork. Modify/reject when added overhead outweighs useful discrimination, late defects erase the gain, or the old visual failure persists without a changed premise. Use inconclusive when evidence/cost comparability is insufficient; name the missing measurement. One iteration cannot establish general causal speedup. Do not invent beauty scores, quotas or percentage savings.
