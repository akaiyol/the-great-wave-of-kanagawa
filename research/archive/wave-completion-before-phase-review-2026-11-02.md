## Decisions and efficiency

Compare artifact to its predicted gain before expanding. Reject obvious failures cheaply; independent review is for a plausible promotion, new method control or unresolved visual diagnosis. Use the same representative failing evidence at intended scale. Run broad exact-regression comparisons once after a shared refactor; reuse their evidence while those owning sources stay unchanged. For local geometry edits, run the affected diagnostics and one discriminating capture first. Ground semantic labels and sky probes in the canonical scan, never in the candidate mask they are meant to test. After two edits preserve the same failure signature without gain, change interpretation/representation/scale; renaming an experiment does not reset the counter.

Before retrying a rejected method, cite its earlier failure and a materially changed premise. No further color-only foam separation or automatic ink-gap closure after W007/W008 unless a new semantic boundary resolves the demonstrated failure. Source reconstruction is a fidelity control, not procedural geometry or semantic segmentation. Research recommendations are hypotheses until tested; do not label them implementation readiness.

Read metadata rather than dumping large geometry/pixel arrays; summarize allowance without account identifiers and batch independent checks. Use the existing working browser/runtime setup, syntax-check before capture, and fail fast on page errors rather than waiting for readiness timeout. Capture one discriminating view first; run broader sizes/checks after visible gain or at a milestone. Do not build new tooling, roles, dashboards or scoring to postpone the visual decision. Node ownership does not turn a generic ink glyph into structural linework: derive creases from actual branch spines. If narrowing a contour waveform removes protrusion, change to centerline-grown branches at the exposed frontier instead of tuning width again. Serialize UI/model code writes; lead captures only held source. Stop unproductive work promptly; do not poll unchanged state repeatedly.

Update the original action-log entry outcome and checkpoint together after review; no stale pending status. Keep one compact iteration entry with evidence, verdict, decision, actual measured usage or unknown, and owned next improvement. Audit repeated failure signatures, artifact delivery, method re-entry and record consistency at milestones; do not duplicate a dashboard/backlog. Workflow changes are unproven until exercised. For water joins, inspect an exposed quiet fixture: no translucent or ghosted crests, reflected chevrons, rectangular joins, or ruler-straight cutoffs. An intentionally soft blend is not source-faithful printed water. Material fidelity is a separate gate: compare paper grain scale, ink saturation/contrast and line finish directly with the canonical scan. Recognition and shape approval do not establish style acceptance. For painted composites, inspect quiet and boundary views as well as the hero landing before promotion; family counts and a successful landing do not establish a continuous ocean. Check actual alpha composites before diagnosing a cutout halo.

Continue authorized work after closeout. Stop at verified release criteria, user request, allowance limit or an external dependency requiring input. Do not use artistic approval as a recurring gate. Boats/Fuji remain undecided but do not block current work.
