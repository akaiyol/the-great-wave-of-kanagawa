# Woodblock validation

Supersedes the Braille protocol on 2026-10-28. The previous protocol is archived in archive/braille-2026-10-28/research/. Existing validation scripts still target the legacy app. This document defines required evidence; it does not claim new checks are implemented or passed.

## Expected output

For the still: verify app loading, finite geometry, intended layer order, unchanged reference pin and captures bound to candidate source, viewport and DPR. Source comparisons must preserve the source crop aspect and coordinate mapping; check rendered canvas proportions when context changes. Ground root/tip anchors and semantic sky probes in the scan, not the candidate mask. Do not regenerate the reference pin.

As procedural scenery and travel land, verify deterministic queries, neighboring-region seams, water/foam/cloud occlusion, regeneration after eviction, bounded caches, controls, pause, reduced motion, hidden-tab handling and desktop resize/DPR. Preserve applicable integrity and interaction checks.

Retire dot occupancy, white-only pixels, empty-sky strips, sampled connectivity and Braille phase offsets from woodblock acceptance.

## Immediate reference recognition

Before inspecting details, view the complete candidate at its intended display size without titles, captions or reference labels. Record whether it immediately evokes The Great Wave, with the specific visible features supporting the judgment. Then compare directly with the canonical scan for fidelity. Generic blue waves, woodblock texture or a recognizable curl alone do not satisfy this criterion.

If the reviewer already knows the project/reference, label this an informed recognizability assessment, not a blind user-recognition test. Do not claim a measured recognition rate or invent a timing threshold. Missing or negative recognition evidence prevents artistic promotion.

## Reference form

Inspect the unchanged scan beside the complete candidate and relevant detail crops at actual display size. Review curling silhouette and proportions; connected unequal foam fingers; water-body continuity and principal hollows; flowing contour bands; indigo outlines and restrained ivory/blue palette; pale clouds, open sky and hierarchy.

Ink marks are not all water cavities. A successful interior crop does not approve a full crest. Texture cannot compensate for weak anatomy. Record observed failures rather than a beauty average or pixel-similarity score.

## Composition and quality

Review at 1280 × 720 and 1920 × 1080 CSS pixels, then narrower desktop resizing when integrated. For scenery milestones include consecutive fixed views and held-out seeds for repetition, transitions, depth and quiet intervals. Record actual browser/DPR and inspected scope; missing evidence is unverified.

Preserve failing comparisons between revisions. Bind captures and verdicts to source/reference identities. Technical success cannot override failed visual form. Independent milestone review is separate from user artistic acceptance.
