# Project goals

User instruction, 2026-11-04. Both goals are equally important. The lead owns delivery, evidence, coherence and publication.

Both goals must satisfy their evidence-based criteria. Passing one cannot compensate for failure of the other. Usage ceilings are session controls, not success criteria. Do not weaken criteria without an explicit user decision.

## ART: Procedural Great Wave ocean

Priority: equal.

Create a visually recognizable Great Wave ocean in the canonical Hokusai woodblock language, using LingDong-style mathematical construction and continuous generation.

Final output: A standalone desktop browser experience with an endlessly explorable generated ocean, pale background clouds, defined connected foam and flowing indigo water.

### A1: Source likeness

The unlabeled whole composition immediately evokes the canonical Great Wave through silhouette, foam anatomy, hollows, contour flow, palette and hierarchy. No texture or title may conceal weak geometry.

Required evidence: Native desktop comparisons with the unchanged canonical scan, independent informed visual review, and separate final user artistic acceptance.

### A2: Procedural construction and variety

Composition, water profiles, crest topology, foam and contours are constructed from geometry. New scenery has materially different anatomy; finished wave sprites, one rescaled template and random decoration do not satisfy this goal.

Required evidence: Source review, seeded reproducibility, new and held-out constructions, normalized geometry comparisons and visual repetition review. Report tested uniqueness scope honestly.

### A3: Coherent continuous ocean

A planner creates wave groups, depth, quiet intervals and clouds. Adjacent regions connect, old locations reproduce after eviction, and working memory remains bounded.

Required evidence: Consecutive travel views, seam and occlusion review, eviction/regeneration checks and a documented cache bound. No untested claim of infinite perceptual uniqueness.

### A4: Usable, defined desktop experience

Slow horizontal travel, pause/manual controls, reduced motion, hidden-tab handling and desktop resizing work. Water and foam remain opaque and connected, without fading joins; rendering remains responsive.

Required evidence: Rendered desktop checks at declared viewports/DPR, interaction and sustained-travel checks, runtime errors, frame timing and memory observations on documented hardware.

## SYSTEM: Efficient evaluated agentic development

Priority: equal.

Develop a small agentic system that reaches the artwork goal through critical thinking, external learning and fast, high-quality experiments with few resources.

Final output: A reusable evidence-driven workflow with independent strategy challenge, technical and visual evaluation, bounded lessons, and explicit continuation and resource controls.

### S1: Sound independent decisions

Challenge assumptions before building. Preserve supported paths, distinguish implementation defects from disproven premises, pivot or reject failed constructions, and defer unsupported judgments.

Required evidence: Causal predictions, supporting/contrary evidence, bounded discriminating tests, independent decisions and prospective outcomes including critic mistakes. A valid JSON record alone is insufficient.

### S2: Efficiency without quality loss

Demonstrate equal or better independently reviewed quality with lower active time and estimated token cost per comparable retained improvement than a declared baseline. Do not trade quality for speed or count activity as progress.

Required evidence: Matched baseline/changed-workflow comparisons with consistent quality criteria; report scope, uncertainty, setup/review overhead and estimate overruns. Separate host-sleep gaps from active work; do not invent exact compute.

### S3: Useful research and durable learning

Learn from relevant primary research and original implementations, transfer tested mechanisms, create new hypotheses and retain evidence-linked lessons. Discard failed active paths and avoid speculative research or an expanding agent fleet without benefit.

Required evidence: Short source/mechanism/limit notes, experimental outcomes, stable failure IDs, changed-premise retry conditions and a bounded startup memory.

### S4: Ownership, continuity and truthful documentation

Every phase stays tied to the two goals, a budget and an observable outcome. Check actual continuation and activity state, respect the user resource ceiling, and update the GitHub README diagram with every architecture change.

Required evidence: Fresh startup retains both goals; strategy/result/process records and resource checkpoints exist; deliberate goal or architecture drift fails checks. State local sleep/runtime limits honestly. The lead owns the complete system.

## Method and reference

[Method inspiration](https://github.com/LingDong-/shan-shui-inf): Composition -> generated object geometry -> surface contours -> attached detail -> vector rendering; seeded construction extends scenery as the viewer travels. Adapt the method to Hokusai; do not substitute mountain motifs or finished wave images.

Canonical reference: references/great-wave-off-kanagawa-met-DP130155.jpg

## Provisional evaluation plan

Starting test plans, not automatic artistic acceptance gates; adjust test scope from observed failures without weakening goal requirements.

ART: Compare whole views at 1280 × 720 and 1920 × 1080, then held-out seeds and consecutive regions; expand to DPR 2 and sustained travel as world integration becomes plausible.

SYSTEM: Use outcome-hidden calibration when feasible, then prospective decisions and matched baseline experiments. Choose sample sizes and timing targets from pilot variability; report when evidence is insufficient.

## Non-goals

- Boats and Fuji remain undecided; they do not block wave, sky, water and cloud work.
- No purchases, quota resets, paid fallback, or unsolicited always-on services.
